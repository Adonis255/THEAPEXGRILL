About Us

Discover the story behind Apex Grill Chepterit

Since 2025

How It All Began

Apex Grill Chepterit was born from a simple idea: create a place where people can enjoy the finest nyama choma while their vehicles receive top-quality care.

Apex Grillhas grown into the premier Choma Zone destination in the region. Our founder, a passionate chef and car enthusiast, saw an opportunity to combine two essential services that the community needed.

Today, we serve over 200 customers daily across our restaurant and car wash operations. Our commitment to quality, cleanliness, and customer satisfaction remains as strong as ever. Every meal is prepared with love, and every car is treated with meticulous care.

Our Core

Mission & Values

Our Mission

To provide an exceptional dining and car care experience that exceeds expectations — serving delicious, high-quality food and delivering spotless vehicles with a smile, every single day.

Our Vision

To become the most loved and trusted Choma Zone and car wash brand in Kenya, known for consistency, innovation, and creating memorable experiences for every guest.

Our Values

Quality, Integrity, Hospitality, Cleanliness, Community, and Continuous Improvement. These six pillars guide everything we do at Apex Grill Chepterit.
