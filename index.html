Our Story

The Heart of Chepterit's
Dining & Car Care

Apex Grill Chepterit is more than a restaurant — it's a destination. Founded with a passion for exceptional dishes and a vision to offer premium car wash services, we've become the go-to spot for families, travelers, and car enthusiasts alike.

Located conveniently on Eldoret - Kapsabet Road in Chepterit, our establishment combines the rich flavors of Kenyan grilling traditions with modern car care technology. Whether you're craving perfectly grilled goat meat or need your vehicle sparkling clean, our friendly team delivers excellence every time.
